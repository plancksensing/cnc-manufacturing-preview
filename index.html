How we review your project
Can I start with just an idea?

Yes. Share a brief, sketch or description of what the product should do. We can discuss design support and agree the scope and deliverables before work begins.

Can I combine several processes in one order?

Yes. Your inquiry can include design, machining, surface finishing and on-part printing. We review them together against the finished product you need.

Can I request a single part?

You can send an inquiry for one part, a prototype batch or a production quantity. We will review feasibility and any process-specific minimums in the quotation.

When do I pay?

After the specifications and quotation are confirmed. Our Hong Kong company provides the invoice and agreed payment instructions.
