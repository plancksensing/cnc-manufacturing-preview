Project review

Based in Hong Kong, Shenzhen and Kunshan, AURVANT combines design and manufacturing expertise to bring your product to life. We work with you to define the details, from the first brief to the finished piece.

Agree on the finished part

Design scope
If you are starting with an idea, agree on the design deliverables and review stages first. We can help develop the model and drawing before the specification is released for manufacturing. Explore design support.
Drawing & revision
Identify the controlling drawing, model and artwork revisions. We clarify critical fits, reference faces and which dimensions apply after finishing.
Appearance approval
Mark the visible faces and specify the color, texture and print position. If approval depends on a physical master, trial piece or artwork proof, agree on that step before production.
Inspection evidence
Tell us which features need recorded measurements, how many pieces to check and what report you expect. Request any material or finish documentation during quotation so availability can be confirmed.
Release & shipment
Agree on the release criteria, any buyer sign-off, protective packaging, delivery destination and shipping terms. Include a required arrival date if your schedule depends on it.

Front panel review

A front panel with a machined opening and printed scale might need these checks.

Fit
Measure the finished instrument opening and its location from the two marked datum edges.
Appearance
Compare the visible blue face to the approved finish sample. Check that the white scale aligns with the opening.
Release
Submit the agreed measurements and a front-view photo for approval before dispatch.

Quotation and order confirmation

Your quotation identifies the Hong Kong contracting entity and sets out the agreed revision, quantity, price, lead time and payment terms. Review those details and any sample or inspection requirements before confirming your order.
