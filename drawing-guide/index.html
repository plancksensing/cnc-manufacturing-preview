Prepare your files

File typeWhat to include
3D modelSTEP / STP or IGES / IGS for part geometry. STL is also accepted. Provide units with mesh files.
DrawingPDF for dimensioned views and process notes. DXF or DWG can also describe flat profiles. Show units, revision and critical features.
ArtworkAI, EPS, SVG or PDF for vector artwork. Include target size, color references and placement on the part.
Visual referencePNG, JPG or JPEG for a marked-up view, existing sample or finish reference. Include dimensions separately.

Attach up to six files. Each file can be up to 10 MiB, with a combined limit of 20 MiB. For a larger package, describe it in your request and arrange a transfer with the team.
