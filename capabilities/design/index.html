From a brief to a defined part

Understand the use
Tell us what the product should do, where it will be used and what it needs to fit. A sketch, reference photo or description is a useful starting point.
Develop the geometry
Work through the shape, overall dimensions and interfaces. We can help turn the agreed direction into a CAD model and dimensioned drawing.
Consider production
Review material options, machining access, critical fits and the effect of finishing. Bring printed artwork and its position into the same discussion when needed.
Agree on the deliverables
Before design work starts, confirm the scope, file formats, review stages, revisions, price and timing. Manufacturing proceeds against the approved specification.
