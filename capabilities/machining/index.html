What defines the part?

Geometry & quantity
Share a STEP model with a dimensioned drawing when available. State the drawing revision, units and number of finished parts.
Material grade
Name the alloy or engineering plastic, including temper or condition when it matters. Indicate whether alternatives can be considered.
Threads & fit
Call out thread size, pitch and usable depth. Identify mating bores, shafts and mounting faces with their functional tolerances.
Inspection
Identify the features you want checked, their reference datums and any reporting requirements. Specify whether dimensions apply before or after finishing.

Example drawing notes

A front panel with an instrument opening and four mounting holes.

Geometry
Size and locate the center opening from two reference edges.
Mounting
Dimension the hole pattern and specify the countersink diameter and angle.
Finish allowance
Identify any bore that must meet its specified fit after finishing.

Does the part need a finish?

A coating can affect fit at a mating surface. Include the finish and any masking requirements with your machining drawing so both stages can be reviewed together.
