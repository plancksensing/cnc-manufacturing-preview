How should the surface perform?

Substrate & condition
Identify the base material and its starting condition. Include a reference photo if existing machining marks or cosmetic faces matter.
Finish specification
Give the process and standard if prescribed. Include color, gloss, texture and thickness requirements, or describe the use and appearance you need.
Masking boundaries
Mark surfaces to keep free of treatment, including threads, contacts and mating faces. Dimension masking edges from a repeatable reference.
Acceptance criteria
Identify visible faces and the conditions for appearance approval. Mention a physical color master or approved comparison sample if available.

A blue anodized aluminum control panel with exposed contact surfaces.

Visible face

Masked areas

Downstream process
White graphics on the front face. Review the finish and print process together.

Will the finished surface carry graphics?

Supply the artwork and placement drawing with the finish request. The substrate, surface texture and printed color all affect the appearance of the finished part.
