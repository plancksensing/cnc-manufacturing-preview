What defines the artwork?

Production artwork
Provide a vector file where possible. Set artwork at its intended size, outline fonts or include font details, and identify each print color.
Print location
Show the printed face in the correct viewing orientation. Dimension the graphic from part features such as a hole center, datum edge or mounting face.
Color & substrate
Give the color reference, base color and surface treatment. For a color match, describe the approved sample or comparison method.
Use & approval
Mention cleaning agents, handling and exposure that affect durability. Specify whether you need a placement proof or a printed sample for approval.

Example print request

A white dial scale aligned to a circular opening on a black panel.

Artwork
One-color vector file, with the scale and labels at finished size.
Registration
Center the scale on the instrument opening. Show the zero mark relative to a reference edge.
Proof
Review the artwork over the face drawing before approving the print position.

Prepare the complete drawing package

Keep the model, drawing and artwork on the same revision. Our drawing guide lists accepted files and the details to include in your request.
